About Shikhar Home Stay

Your home away from home in the city of Lord Shiva

🏠

Our Story

Welcome to Shikhar Home Stay, where tradition meets comfort. Established with a vision to provide pilgrims with a peaceful and comfortable stay in the holy city of Ujjain, we have been serving travelers from across India and around the world.

Our bungalow is more than just accommodation – it's a sanctuary where you can rest, rejuvenate, and connect with the spiritual energy of Ujjain, the city of temples and divine presence.

Located just minutes away from the sacred Mahakaleshwar Temple, our property offers the perfect base for your pilgrimage to one of India's most sacred cities.

We understand the needs of pilgrims who travel long distances to seek blessings at the Jyotirlinga. Our mission is to make your stay as comfortable and hassle-free as possible, so you can focus on your spiritual journey.

👤

Meet Your Hosts

We are a local Ujjain family who has lived in this sacred city for generations. We understand the importance of pilgrimage and strive to make every guest feel like a part of our family. Our knowledge of local temples, rituals, and traditions helps us guide our guests for a meaningful spiritual experience.
