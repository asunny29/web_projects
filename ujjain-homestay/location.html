Our bungalow is centrally located in Ujjain, making it easy to visit all the major temples and attractions. We're just minutes away from Mahakaleshwar Temple.

🚂

Nearby Temples & Attractions

Explore the sacred sites of Ujjain

🕉️

Mahakaleshwar Temple

1 km - One of the 12 Jyotirlingas in India. Famous for Bhasma Aarti.

🕉️

Kal Bhairav Temple

2 km - Temple dedicated to deity Kal Bhairav, open 24/7.

🕉️

Ram Ghat

3 km - Sacred ghat on Kshipra river, famous for evening aarti.

🕉️

Mangalnath Temple

4 km - Temple of Mangal (Mars), believed to be birthplace of Mars.

🕉️

Harsiddhi Temple

2 km - One of the 51 Shakti Peethas, dedicated to Goddess Harsiddhi.
